This policy explains what data the Qbit Remote app (the "app") handles, why, and what your rights are. The app is a remote control for your own qBittorrent client with its web interface (WebUI): downloads are done by your server, not by your phone.

Controller

Appsconarte, the app developer. Contact: infodeicer@gmail.com

What we do NOT do

  • We have no servers of our own that receive your data, your torrents or your qBittorrent traffic.
  • We require no registration or account of any kind.
  • We do not send your server address, username or password to the cloud.
  • We do not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

1. Connection to your qBittorrent

The app connects directly, from your phone, to the address (IP or name), port, username and password that you enter. That traffic goes only between your phone and your server; it does not pass through us. If your server uses plain HTTP, the traffic is not encrypted inside your network: use HTTPS or a VPN when connecting from outside your home.

2. Data stored on your phone

The app stores only on your device, in the app private storage: the name, address, port, username and password of your servers, your app preferences, and whether you bought "Remove ads". These data are not backed up to the cloud and none of it reaches us.

3. .torrent files and links

If you add a torrent from a file or a link, the app sends it to your qBittorrent server. We do not read it or store it anywhere else.

4. Advertising

The app is free thanks to advertising: a small banner and, occasionally, a full-screen ad after adding torrents. Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited), which may process your device advertising ID, IP address, technical device data and your interactions with ads, in order to show and measure them and prevent fraud.

The first time you open the app we ask for your consent through the Google form. If you do not consent, ads will be non-personalised. You can change your choice in the app, under Options › Ad privacy options, and reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings. More information: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

5. Purchases

"Remove ads" is paid through Google Play. Google handles the payment under its own terms; we do not receive your payment details, only confirmation that the purchase exists.

Legal basis

Personalised advertising is based on your consent (GDPR art. 6.1.a), which you can withdraw at any time. Purchase handling is based on performance of a contract (art. 6.1.b).

How to delete your Qbit Remote data

  1. Everything stored on your phone (servers, passwords and preferences): in Android, Settings › Apps › Qbit Remote › Storage › Clear data, or uninstall the app. You can also remove each server from within the app, under Servers.
  2. Advertising data: in the app, Ad privacy options to withdraw your consent, and in Android, Settings › Privacy › Ads › Delete advertising ID. Data already processed by Google AdMob is kept according to the Google policy (policies.google.com/technologies/retention).
  3. If you prefer to ask us: write to infodeicer@gmail.com with the subject "Delete data – Qbit Remote". We will reply within 30 days.

What is retained: nothing in our systems, because we do not receive your data. Your purchases remain recorded in your Google Play account, which keeps them under its own policy, so you can restore them if you reinstall the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to infodeicer@gmail.com. For advertising and purchase data you may also contact Google. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).
